# Kenney Car Kit assets

`truck.glb`, `police.glb`, `sedan.glb`, `suv.glb`, and `cone.glb` are from `Models/GLB format/` in the mirror https://github.com/Arslan12216775/kenney_car-kit at commit `153591d606970058a4d0e44aeadf435c2d3f89ed`. The repository's `License.txt` identifies **Car Kit 3.1**, by Kenney, as CC0; its text is copied here. GLB files contain embedded color textures and wheel meshes. They are stylized low-poly art, not photorealistic PBR scans, and are used for the traffic blockout. Transforms and lighting are applied at runtime.
