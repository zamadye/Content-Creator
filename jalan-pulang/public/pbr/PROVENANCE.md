# PBR texture sources

- `street_asphalt_*` and `metal_rusty_paint_*`: https://github.com/itz019/tls-textures/tree/a6be37350995fe094b3ee94baffba8154fd86040. The repository identifies the source as ambientCG and its maps as CC0 1.0; it notes AO is baked into color. Original asset IDs are not identified by the mirror.
- Color textures are sRGB; normal, roughness, metalness and AO are linear data. No texture here is claimed to be an authored texture for this specific model.
- `sidewalk_concrete_*` and `wall_plaster_dirty_*`: same ambientCG mirror and revision as above. Mirror labels the files CC0; original asset IDs are not specified.
- `wood_planks_*`: https://github.com/nfranchetti-svg/abitaprima-assets/tree/c9c9649beff61aca562647814b953b059bf5754c/textures/wood_planks. Mirror README says its textures are Poly Haven, CC0, and normal maps OpenGL. Individual original asset identity/licence not independently verified; retain this caveat until checked. Images were not modified.
