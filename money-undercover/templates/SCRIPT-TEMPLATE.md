## Writing Rules (QC-enforced)

- Every scene advances the story; every scene answers a question or creates a new one.
- No unnecessary repetition, no excessive definitions, no artificially delayed obvious answers.
- Tension built through evidence, not fabricated suspense.
- No generic AI writing patterns, no repetitive rhetorical questions.
- No exaggerated factual claims.
- Every important claim maps to a research ledger ID.
- Distinguish FACT / INTERPRETATION / OPINION / HYPOTHESIS where relevant.
- No personalized investment recommendations.
- Natural, conversational but authoritative investigative delivery.
