## Animation Direction (per scene type)

| Intent | Motion approach |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Slow camera movement, subtle reveal |
| Explanation | Controlled motion graphics, sequential diagrams |
| Conflict | Faster movement, stronger framing, dynamic transitions |
| Reveal | Clear focal point, controlled pause, dramatic sound accent |

**Forbidden:** continuous camera shake · excessive zoom · unmotivated particles · random transitions · motion that harms readability.
